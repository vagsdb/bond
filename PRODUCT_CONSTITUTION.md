# Bond Product Constitution

Bond exists to create meaningful human encounters, not to maximize attention.

## Core promise

Find people who may matter to each other for reasons they could not easily have specified themselves.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. No infinite feed.
2. No swiping.
3. No follower counts.
4. No public like counts or popularity scores.
5. No ranking people by attractiveness or engagement.
6. No artificial daily matching quota.
7. No retention notifications without genuine social value.
8. No precise-location discovery.
9. No visible rejection mechanics.
10. No paid boosts or monetized exposure.
11. A shorter session may represent greater product success.
12. Every feature must be evaluated against one question: does it increase the probability of a meaningful human encounter?

## Matching philosophy

Bond should search for:

- shared core
- interesting divergence
- reciprocal relevance
- conversational compatibility
- current social intention
- timing
- geographic feasibility

Bond should penalize:

- explicit exclusions
- oversupplied social worlds
- superficial similarity
- asymmetric benefit
- repeated introductions
- weak or unsafe contexts

## Product success

The system should ultimately learn from relationship outcomes rather than attention metrics:

introduction → mutual acceptance → conversation → offline meeting → glad-we-met → continued contact

The signature moment is not a match notification. It is the later realization:

> I would never have met this person otherwise.
